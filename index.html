Story Studio Publisher

Story Studio Publisher is a private, single-owner tool that schedules and publishes short videos from a small independent studio to the studio's own TikTok accounts (for example Wholesome True Stories and Indian Mythology).

What it does

  • The account owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes the tool for their own account.
  • The tool uploads finished videos that the studio produced, with a caption, at a planned time, using TikTok's Content Posting API.
  • It reads basic post information (status, view counts) so the studio can see how its own videos perform.

The tool is not offered to the public, does not post on behalf of anyone else, and does not collect data about other TikTok users.
